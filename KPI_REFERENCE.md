# Tahlil Platform - KPI Analysis Capabilities
## Complete List of Supported Key Performance Indicators

The Tahlil platform can analyze and calculate any KPI from your data. Simply upload your data files and ask questions about these metrics.

## 📊 R&D Metrics

### Cost & Budget KPIs
- **R&D Spend as a Percentage of Revenue** - Calculate R&D expenses divided by total revenue
- **Cost Per Feature Developed** - Total R&D cost divided by number of features
- **Development Budget Adherence** - Actual vs. planned budget comparison
- **Resource Allocation Efficiency** - How effectively R&D resources are used
- **Cost Overrun Percentage** - Percentage by which actual costs exceed budget
- **Feature-to-Product Cost Ratio** - Cost of features vs. total product cost
- **Development Rework Rate** - Percentage of work that needs to be redone
- **Average Development Cost per Hour** - Total development cost divided by hours worked

### Time & Efficiency KPIs
- **Average Time-to-Market** - Time from concept to product launch
- **Development Cycle Time** - Time to complete development cycle
- **Percentage of On-Time Launches** - Projects launched on schedule
- **Iteration Time per Product** - Time between product iterations
- **Time-to-Prototyping** - Time to create first prototype
- **Time-to-Testing** - Time to reach testing phase
- **Launch Readiness Index** - Readiness score before launch

### Quality & Performance KPIs
- **Team Utilization Rate** - Percentage of team capacity used
- **Resource Turnover Rate** - Rate of resource changes
- **Bottleneck Identification Rate** - How quickly bottlenecks are found
- **Delay Rate** - Percentage of delayed projects
- **Average Approval Time** - Time for approvals

## 📊 How to Use Tahlil for KPI Analysis

### Step 1: Prepare Your Data
Upload data files containing:
- Financial data (revenue, costs, expenses)
- Operational data (production, inventory, logistics)
- Customer data (purchases, engagement, satisfaction)
- Marketing data (campaigns, ads, social media)
- Project data (tasks, timelines, budgets)
- HR data (productivity, utilization, engagement)

### Step 2: Ask Questions About KPIs

**Example Questions:**

**Financial KPIs:**
- "What is our gross profit margin?"
- "Calculate revenue growth rate for the last quarter"
- "Show me cost per unit by product"
- "What is our break-even point?"

**Operational KPIs:**
- "Calculate inventory turnover rate"
- "What is our on-time delivery rate?"
- "Show me production cycle time by product"
- "What is our capacity utilization rate?"

**Customer KPIs:**
- "Calculate customer lifetime value"
- "What is our customer retention rate?"
- "Show me customer acquisition cost by channel"
- "What is our net promoter score?"

**Marketing KPIs:**
- "Calculate return on ad spend (ROAS)"
- "What is our email open rate?"
- "Show me cost per lead by campaign"
- "What is our conversion rate?"

**Project KPIs:**
- "Calculate budget variance for all projects"
- "What is our on-time completion rate?"
- "Show me cost overrun percentage"
- "What is our project success rate?"

### Step 3: Get Formatted Results
- **Tables** - KPI values in organized tables
- **Charts** - Visual representations of KPIs over time
- **Explanations** - Natural language descriptions of what KPIs mean

---

## 💡 Tips for KPI Analysis

1. **Include Time Periods** - Ask for KPIs over specific timeframes
2. **Compare Periods** - "Compare Q1 vs Q2 revenue growth"
3. **Segment Data** - "Show customer retention by region"
4. **Trend Analysis** - "Show monthly inventory turnover trends"
5. **Benchmarking** - "Compare our metrics to industry averages"
